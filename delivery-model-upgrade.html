Delivery Model Upgrade

For growing B2B SaaS companies whose implementation delivery model no longer fits the business.

In a focused 6–8 week engagement, we diagnose where delivery is getting stuck and design a practical model for your next stage of growth. The work clarifies roles, responsibilities, decision rights, handoffs, capacity, customer inputs, metrics and tools, and team requirements.

We look at how work moves from sale through implementation and into customer success: who owns each decision, what teams and customers need to contribute, where handoffs break down, and whether capacity and measures support the outcomes you expect.

The result is a delivery model your leaders and team can use to make clearer decisions, coordinate work, and plan for growth.

Engagement at a glance

Timing
6–8 weeks

Working sessions
Up to four focused sessions with your team

Take-away
Blueprint Execution Plan: a practical delivery model with prioritized next steps

What we examine

We work with your leaders and delivery team to understand where the current model creates friction, then design how the work should flow as the company grows.

  • Roles, responsibilities, and decision rights
  • Sales, implementation, and customer success handoffs
  • Delivery capacity and workload assumptions
  • Customer inputs and readiness needed for delivery
  • Metrics and tools for visibility and decisions
  • Team capabilities and requirements for the next stage

Blueprint Execution Plan

You leave with a practical, prioritized plan for putting the delivery model in place: recommended actions, sequence, ownership, and decisions to resolve. Up to four focused working sessions give your team time to review the diagnosis, make key choices, and align around the plan.

Designed for your team to own

You leave with a practical delivery model, shared decisions, and a prioritized plan for putting it in place. Your team keeps ownership of implementation and can adapt the plan to the way your business works.

The engagement focuses on model design and planning. Your team leads the follow-through, including system configuration, final operational materials, recruiting or managing staff, and running delivery.
