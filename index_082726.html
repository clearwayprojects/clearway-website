The Reinvention Cycle

The Revenue Stuck in Delivery Problem

Sales is doing its job. Contracts are getting signed. But customers aren't reaching value quickly enough—and revenue is effectively sitting in implementation.

I help shorten time-to-value by fixing the gaps between sales, implementation, and customer success.

The Leadership Gap

You know implementation needs stronger leadership, but you're not ready to hire a full-time executive. Or you need someone experienced now while you figure out what the organization should become.

That's where fractional leadership comes in. I can step into the operation, stabilize delivery, lead the team, and build what you need for the next stage.

For Buyers:

The "We'll Just Figure It Out" Trap

It's fintech, so you put Sarah in Accounting in charge of the rollout. She's smart and capable. But she also has her actual job. Three months later, nothing's happened because she has zero time for this.

I help you get realistic about bandwidth and build a plan that actually works

The Decision Deadlock

Your team knows what needs to happen, but they can't get decisions from the customer. The people empowered to decide aren't invested. The people who care can't make the call.

I teach your team how to surface decisions early and get stakeholders accountable

The Stakeholder Standoff

Sales wants it yesterday. IT says it can't be done. Operations is threatening to build a workaround in Excel. Nobody's talking to each other.

I facilitate the hard conversations that actually resolve conflict

The Shelfware Risk

You spent $50K on software. Three months after go-live, nobody's using it. People are back to the old system and you can't figure out why.

I design adoption strategies that stick beyond the launch party
