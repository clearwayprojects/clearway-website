Overview

At ClearWay Projects, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information. Our goal is to be transparent and responsible with any data we handle.

Information We Collect

We do not use cookies, analytics tools, or tracking technologies on our website.
We only collect information in the following limited situations:

  • When you voluntarily contact us through our website, email, or social media.
  • When you choose to subscribe to updates or request information from us.
The information you provide may include your name, email address, company name, and details about your project or inquiry.

How We Use Your Information

  • Responding to your inquiries or service requests.
  • Providing project-related information or proposals.
  • Managing ongoing client relationships.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Data is stored securely using reputable cloud-based tools and is only accessible to ClearWay Projects team members who need it to perform their work.

Third-Party Services

Currently, we do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or plugins that collect visitor information. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To make such a request, please contact us using the details below.
