Services for SaaS Providers

Stronger delivery. Scalable processes. Better customer outcomes.

As B2B SaaS companies grow, the delivery practices that worked at an earlier stage can become a constraint. Implementation takes longer, teams struggle with increasing complexity, and customers wait too long to realize value.

I help SaaS companies identify what's holding delivery back, redesign the operating model and processes to support growth, and build the capabilities needed for more predictable implementation. Depending on the challenge, that may mean a focused assessment and plan, hands-on process redesign, or experienced leadership for the implementation function.

Delivery Model Upgrade

When growth outpaces your delivery model, something has to change.

I work with SaaS leaders to identify the constraints affecting implementation performance and design a delivery model that can support the next stage of growth. The engagement examines how work flows across teams, where capacity and accountability break down, and what needs to change in processes, roles, metrics, and decision-making.

You leave with a prioritized Blueprint Execution Plan that gives your team a clear path from diagnosis to action.

Scalable Process Architecture

Build implementation processes that keep pace with growth.

As your customer base grows, informal workflows, inconsistent handoffs, and manual coordination can turn implementation into a bottleneck. Scalable Process Architecture redesigns how work moves through your implementation operation, creating repeatable workflows, clear accountability, and consistent communication without adding unnecessary complexity.

I work with your team to assess existing processes, redesign critical workflows, develop practical tools and standards, and support rollout and training. The result is a more consistent, predictable implementation experience that can scale with your business.

Fractional Implementation Leadership

Experienced leadership without adding a full-time executive.

When growth outpaces your implementation team's capacity or leadership, delivery can become harder to manage and improve. I provide experienced implementation leadership to help stabilize delivery, strengthen team performance, and build the structure needed to support continued growth.

The goal is a stronger implementation function that delivers consistently and is equipped for the company's next stage of growth.

Typical areas of focus include:

  • Leading and developing implementation and professional services teams
  • Improving sales-to-implementation handoffs
  • Establishing delivery processes, metrics, and operating rhythms
  • Building capacity and resource planning
  • Improving project predictability and delivery margins
  • Shortening time-to-value
  • Identifying what the organization needs to support its next stage of growth

Implementation Health Check

A focused assessment to identify what needs attention first.

Not every delivery challenge requires a full-scale engagement. The Implementation Health Check is a quick, focused review based on the Professional Services Scaling Scorecard—not a comprehensive operational assessment or redesign.

In a one-hour working session, I discuss the scorecard results with your team, explore what's behind the biggest concerns, and identify the areas most in need of attention. Within a week, you'll receive a concise written summary of my findings and prioritized recommendations.

It's a practical, low-commitment way to get an experienced outside perspective on your implementation operation and determine where to focus next.
